Synthetic demo. Everything on this site is built on made-up data. No real pensioner, no real government feed and no production data is connected.

Find pensions that keep paying after a death, without stopping anyone's pension by mistake

When a pensioner dies and nobody reports it, the pension can keep being paid for years. The Pension Integrity Monitor ranks pensioners whose records suggest a death may have gone unreported, so a human officer can check the case. It orders a review queue. It never stops a pension by itself.

How a case moves

  1. Feeds in. Department records (pension roll, death registry, life certificates, bank and telecom activity, hospital, electoral and others) are loaded by staff as CSV files, each checked against a documented schema.
  2. Score and rank. Each pensioner gets a review-priority score from the evidence for and against a death. Quiet records alone never convict: silence without a sharp stop is capped.
  3. Human review. An officer works the case through registrar cross-check, field visit, family contact and bank confirmation. Reasons are written down.
  4. Two people decide. A different supervisor must approve. A death is final only after a notice and appeal period, and can be reopened.
  5. Families can appeal. The notice carries a reference and a deadline. Payment continues provisionally while an independent supervisor reviews the appeal.
  6. Everything is logged in a tamper-evident, hash-chained audit trail.

Human in the loop

Flags only order a queue. No hold, stop or payment change happens without verification steps, written reasons and a second approver.

Appeals built in

Families get a public portal with a one-time code, a tracking reference and a fixed deadline. A release calculates full arrears for every day held.

Open evidence

The benchmark generator, the pre-committed mix declaration and hashes are public, so the result can be reproduced.

What this demo is, and is not

  • Synthetic data only. The benchmark uses generated records with deliberately impossible identifiers, so they can never be mistaken for real ones.
  • No real-world accuracy has been measured. No production data has been scored. The benchmark numbers describe a synthetic test, not performance on real pensioners.
  • Manual pipeline. Staff run the scoring pipeline by hand. It does not rescore automatically when a record arrives.
  • No payments are sent. A release records a local state change and calculates arrears.
  • Staff pages need a named staff account. There is no shared or demo login. The staff dashboard asks you to sign in.

The benchmark measures a separate frozen research detector. It is shown on its own page and is not the score this app computes. Public data context lists real, published aggregate statistics with sources.
