This is not the score the app shows

The figures above measure the frozen V15 research detector on synthetic feeds. The app in this demo uses its own configurable likelihood-ratio scorer to rank a review queue. The benchmark result is not inherited by the app, and the app's queue has not been measured on real data. The app's scores are review priority, not the chance that someone has died.

Hard cases are included: 1,496 targets died within 45 days of the snapshot, 1,903 have no registered death record, and 385 are both. V15 caught 147 of those 385.

How it was kept honest

  1. The detector was frozen on 4 Nov 2026 at 16:28 IST, before the set existed.
  2. The data mix was declared and hashed before any generation.
  3. The set was generated once from a published generator and seed.
  4. The frozen detector was scored once. Nothing was tuned on this set.
  5. Regenerating from the published files reproduces all 29 feeds byte for byte.

Rejected candidates and their numbers are published too.
