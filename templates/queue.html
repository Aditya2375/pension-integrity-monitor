Ranked by calibrated probability. Priority line: p* = {{ '%.2f'|format(p_star) }} from the configured costs. This queue never holds a pension; officers do, after verification, with written reasons.

Tier 1: 99.13% precision, 88.81% recall (blind-verified frozen benchmark). Of 1,000 tier-1 reviews about 991 confirm a death. tier 1 cases sit above p*: this is the number that describes the system.

Speed. Run the pipeline daily. About 99.7% of tier-1 flags are documentary (registry, hospital, electoral), so under daily scoring a flag fires 1-2 days after the first evidence record lands. The remaining wait is upstream: in the synthetic data the first evidence record itself arrives a median of about 20 days after death (registration lag); no scoring design can flag before any signal exists.

tier 2 is an optional broad-review band below p*, for spare reviewer capacity only. Its numbers are separate and must not be blended with tier 1: on local synthetic sets it adds a few recall points at under 1% band precision (about 8 confirmations per 1,000 tier-2 reviews, band covering roughly half the roll). It is never evidence by itself and never changes what a hold requires. Tier 2 figures are local synthetic measurements, not blind-verified.
